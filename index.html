A little about me

A bit about me

I’m Declan. I like 3D modeling, games, creativity, and engineering. With 3D modeling, it’s mostly the mechanical stuff that interests me.

I have a 3D printer and I’ve made a pizza game. I also do fencing and love basketball, so this website has a bit of both making things and sports.

A closer look

A bit more about my interests.

01 / 3D modeling

The mechanical stuff

I like 3D modeling because of the mechanical stuff. I’m interested in parts that fit together and mechanisms that move.

That’s also where my interest in engineering comes in. It’s about how something works as well as what it looks like.

I have a 3D printer too, so I can take a model off the screen and print it.

02 / Games

Playing and making

Games are another thing I’m into. I wanted this website to have things you can actually play with, like the gears and basketball game.

I’ve also made A Pizza Game. You can open it using the link on this page.

03 / Creativity

Coming up with ideas

I like creativity, whether it’s an idea for a model or something for a game.

Making stuff gives me a way to try those ideas out.

04 / Engineering

How things work

I’m into engineering, especially the way parts and mechanisms work together.

That’s why it fits with my interest in mechanical 3D modeling and printing.
